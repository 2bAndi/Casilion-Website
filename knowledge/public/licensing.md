Using Cassilion is free. Support is what the paid plans add.

## Plans

The Community plan costs nothing and comes without support. The paid plans are yearly support plans. Prices and what each plan includes are on the pricing page.

## The license file

Every account gets a license file, `cassilion.lic`. A license is bound to a cluster and states the edition, the capacity, the term and the features it covers. The cluster verifies the file's signatures offline; it never contacts a license server.

## When a plan ends

A support plan and a license are two things. When a paid support plan ends, the account returns to the Community plan and support ends; the license of a cluster has its own term. Renew a license before its term ends: an expired license first puts the cluster into a read-only grace period.
