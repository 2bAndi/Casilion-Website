What support means here

Support starts with Cassilion's support AI. It reads your ticket against the documentation and the product's source code and answers usage and configuration questions directly. Its replies are marked as written by an AI.

When a ticket looks like a defect in Cassilion, the engineering team is told at once, on every plan. Reports of data loss, corruption and security problems always go to a person.

On the Community plan there are no tickets, but you can send a bug report. Bug reports are read; they do not get an individual reply.
